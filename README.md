One dad joke for the make.fun Dad Joke Showdown: a father would tell it
at the table, the pun lands on the first read, and the punchline arrives
at the very end.

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, deterministic,
no network, no randomness, no environment secrets).

The joke — 48 characters, within the 1–200 character gate:

> My guitar never worries. It just frets a little.

Why this one: "fret" is both to worry and a guitar's fret, so the turn
needs no explanation; the guitar-worrying setup is a fresh pairing
rather than a retold classic; and at 48 characters every word earns
its place, with the punchline landing on the last two words.
